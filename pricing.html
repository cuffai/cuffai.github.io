Questions

Do I need a card for the Free plan?

No. Download the app or open Cuff AI Web and start. Free stays free.

What does Pro give me on the desktop and in Chrome?

The desktop app and Chrome work fully on Free. Pro adds Cuff's own AI in Cuff AI Web, so you don't need a key, and in Chrome it lets you approve protected sites from your phone and keeps your full history. Sign in in either app to show Pro there.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time, from "Manage plan" in Cuff AI Web or in the desktop app. It opens Stripe's billing page, where you can cancel.

How is a company billed?

Per person in your organisation, each month or each year. During early access every organisation gets Team free, and admins get an email 30 days before anything is charged.

Who counts as a person?

Anyone who is an active member of your organisation, whatever their role. Each person can enrol up to 3 computers.

Does Cuff see my files or prompts?

No. Cuff runs on your computer. The company console only receives what each computer reports: its name, its agents and the policy it applied.
