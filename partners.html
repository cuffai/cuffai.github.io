Offer your clients control over their AI agents

For managed IT and security providers. Founding partner terms for the first 10 providers.

Your clients' staff already run AI agents like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor on company computers. These agents can read files, run commands and use cloud keys, and antivirus and device management weren't built to limit what they do. Cuff AI lets you find every agent the scan can see, set one dial for what agents may do, stop them in one click, and keep a tamper-evident audit log. It sits alongside the security stack you already manage and works with any agent vendor.

How it works

  1. Talk to us. Email info.cuffai@gmail.com. We'll set up free seats for your team and a 30-minute onboarding call.
  2. Pilot at one client. We help you run a free 30-day pilot for up to 50 people.
  3. Go live. You bill your client; we send you one monthly invoice listing each client and its active people.

What you get today

  • An agent scan on Mac and Windows that finds AI agents, agents set to start by themselves, and the tools wired into apps like Claude Desktop, Cursor and VS Code.
  • One company dial for what agents may do, signed so a computer can't be fed a fake policy.
  • A one-click stop for every agent the scan found, with each computer confirming.
  • A tamper-evident, hash-chained audit log, exportable as JSON or CSV.
  • Single sign-on with Entra ID, Okta or Google Workspace, SCIM provisioning, and data stored in Canada (Montreal).

What isn't built yet

We'd rather you hear it from us. Each client gets its own Cuff organisation, and you switch between them in the console; a single view across all your clients isn't built yet. There's no consolidated billing in the product yet (we invoice you by hand), and no PSA or RMM integrations. Approvals, live activity and SIEM export are in development. Founding partners will shape what we build first. More on our security page.
