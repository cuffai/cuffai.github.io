Seven steps from Off to Full trust.

You decide how much your AI can do. Lower the dial any time, and anything it's running stops right away.

Walls it can't climb.

Cuff uses the protections built into your operating system, not just polite instructions to the AI.

Secrets stay hidden

Passwords, SSH keys, cloud logins and browser profiles are invisible to agents unless you choose Full trust.

Undo anything

Edits wait on a copy for your review. When real files change, a restore point is saved first.

Internet on your terms

Agents only reach the sites you allow. Anything that sends data out needs your yes, once.

Works with the AI you already use.

Start it from Cuff with one click, or add your own bot. Each runs inside the same walls.

Your phone is the remote.

Scan a code once. Then see what your AI is doing, approve requests or hit Stop from anywhere. Everything between your phone and computer is end-to-end encrypted. Our relay only ever sees scrambled bytes.

Full trust can only be turned on at the computer, so a lost phone can't hand your machine to an agent.
